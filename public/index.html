Our Services

We work as a common destination for people willing to contribute and the people who are looking forward for some help,specially in the field of medical supplies.
We work in accordance to the basic principle of humanity, that is to allevate sufferings of fellow beings wherever and whenever we can and ensuring respect for everyone's dignity.

At Green Leaf, a donor can avail medecines and other medical equipments like wheelchair, arm slings, walking sticks and many more, used or unused, which may no longer be of use to the donor but can be very useful for the people who may not be able to afford these supplies and are in need of such medecines and equipments.

Any person in need of any medical equipment can browse throughout the list of donated items and also items available for sale, with search results being specific to the user's city and Green Leaf also provides the users with information about N.G.Os in their city, by contacting these the person can look for the medecines and equipments they are in need of.

After registering through government certified documents, NGOs can search for medecines and medical equipments throughout the available items listed as donation or put up for sale, with search results being specific to the locality of NGOs and procure them in the quantity required by them for judicial and responsible distribution of such supplies to people in need.
